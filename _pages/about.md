Hello, 

my name is Fabian and I am an astrophysicist. I have a passion for space exploration and the universe.

I am a PhD student at the RWTH Aachen University in the group of <a href="https://web.physik.rwth-aachen.de/user/pmertsch/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Philipp Mertsch</a> and also part of the <a href="ttps://mw-atlas.eu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">mw-atlas</a> project. Currently, I work on the distribution of gas and dust in the Milky Way and the correlation between the two. To study this, I use methods of Bayesian inference and Information Field Theory. 

Prior to that, I was a Master student at the University of Vienna in the group of <a href="https://joaoalves.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">João Alves</a>. There I worked on the timescales of protoplanetary disc evolution and dispersion (and I still do occasionally). There I caught my interest in methods of Bayesian inference and machine learning and I enjoy applying these methods to astrophysical problems ever since. 

On my webpage, you will find all information regarding my academic career. In the tabs [Publications](/publications), my published papers are listed. In [Talks](/talks) my recent talks and posters in various locations can be seen (also check out the map of all the places I've been to with my research) and in [Teaching](/teaching) my teaching and supervision experience is summarized. Lastly, you can find everything summarized in my CV and also download it for further information. 

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out to me!
