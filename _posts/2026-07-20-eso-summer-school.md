During the 20th of July 2026 and for the full week, I went back to the European Southern Observatory to attend the [ESO Summer School of Writing and Communicating your Science](https://www.eso.org/sci/meetings/2026/science_writing26.html). In this summer school, we learned how to give good presentations, write papers using the techniques from story telling, and how to present our posters in a quick and catchy way. The end was marked by a show at the ESO Supernova. I learned a lot about communicating my science better to various audiences and had great fun overall.
