In the week of the 27th of July 2026, I ventured on a journey to Exeter, England, to attend the [Discs on the Exe](https://discsontheexe.org/). There I presented my paper on the disc lifetimes in the Scorpius-Centaurus OB association in the disc dispersial plenary session; my slides are [here](/talks). I had an amazing time there, connecting for me for the first time with the community of protoplanetary discs and I hope I can continue with that in the future.
