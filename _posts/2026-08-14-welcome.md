After publishing my first paper and many discussions with colleagues, I was often urged to create my own webpage to present my research here. And now I finally did it (I hope they are happy with the results). So welcome on my page, where you can explore all of my research, where I present it, and what I teach to the next generation of researchers. You will also find my github account, my CV, and various ways of reaching out to me if you have anything to chat about! 

I plan to use this blog to highlight presentations and talks I give, and journeys I take as part of my research. I've already given some talks in my short career, which can be found in the [Talks](/talks) section of this page. Furthermore, I plan to dedicate a post for all of these conferences and meetings to summarize what happened over the next few weeks. So stay tuned for a summary of the past and what's next to come.
