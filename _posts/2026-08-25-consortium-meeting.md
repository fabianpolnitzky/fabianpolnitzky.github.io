In the week of the 24th of August 2026, our collaboration had its annual consortium meeting, this time at our university in Aachen. For the full week, all PhD students presented their current work, externals showed what they are working on and how it can help us, and we had many discussion overall about science. Lastly, we took a trip to the Effelsberg Radio Telescope in the Eifel nearby for a tour of the facility and a hike afterwards.
