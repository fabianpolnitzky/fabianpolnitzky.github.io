During the week of the 21st September 2026, I went to Rethymno in Crete, Greece, for the [Star Formation Fields Conference](https://star-formation-fields.univie.ac.at/). Together with my colleagues from mw-atlas, I presented the current state of my research on the combined inference of the gas and dust structure for the Milky Way and how we use simulations to test our forwards models; for more check [here](/talks). It was a great week full of discussions, and I am glad that I was a part of it.
